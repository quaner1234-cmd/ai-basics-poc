**Demo video:** https://youtu.be/Ocvk4cys31A — 0:57, Unlisted, watchable without a login (checked 2026-10-21)

## Story

### Inspiration

I joined this contest because I enjoyed another building contest, the WeChat Mini Program contest. I learnt a lot and it was fun, so I asked ChatGPT if there were others it could recommend. Then I came here.

I have to say, at first I thought it would be too easy, as this is AI Basics, but I have built something.

### What it does

The page is about AI and crime. I created this page with pixel fonts and a terminal-style typing reveal to show what AI does when it meets real violence. I want people to change their thinking from "AI hurts people" to what AI actually did, using a real case. This page is mainly for people who know little about AI.

### How I built it

In the process, it was interesting, because I had never built something as a complete beginner. I followed the skill instructions to think before acting. This was a bit difficult for me, as I usually don't think too much when I build things. But I followed the agent's instructions in the skill and finally built a simple page. I edited a video and held off submitting. I did not submit immediately, and in the following days, this experience stayed with me when I built other things.

### What I learned

I love sharing with people when I do something successfully, but the truth is many people are still at a very early stage of using AI or agents, which means they need help. When I decided to share my experience, this contest came to mind. So I packed part of what I learned here into my public GitHub repos to help those who are very green at building or learning with AI/agents, and to let agents guide them to the end. This is very important, because when I guided my friends to use some workflows, they did not even know what a terminal was, just like me at the beginning. So this skill pack helps them do it smoothly.

You can check the repositories here:

- https://github.com/quaner1234-cmd/labor-arbitration-toolkit-cn
- https://github.com/quaner1234-cmd/trade-client-memory

### Closing

Anyway, I am really happy that I decided to join this. Thanks a lot.
